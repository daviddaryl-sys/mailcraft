# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who work through email throughout the day, often with more than one mailbox. The desktop app keeps mail, calendars, contacts and tasks close together. People coming from Outlook should recognise where to search, write, reply and file mail.

## Product Purpose

Give people a native, private workspace for daily correspondence and planning, including Despres Connect and independent mail providers. The interface succeeds when people can tell which folder and message they are working in, scan a dense inbox quickly and reach routine commands without breaking their flow.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise and familiar. Speak plainly, especially when an account needs attention or a message action has consequences. The Despres Connect folded envelope gives the app its own identity.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic dashboard cards, decorative gradients, giant account setup buttons and an exact visual copy of Outlook. Use the familiar correspondence workflow with MailCraft's own palette, icons and components.

## Design Principles

- Keep folder navigation, the message list and reading in a stable layout.
- Give the most common action a clear place; make account setup available without dominating the folder tree.
- Let people scan sender, subject, date and status at a glance with more than color alone.
- Protect keyboard, folder resizing, search scope and multi-account context as first-class workflows.
- Use synthetic mail to evaluate the interface; never expose a real mailbox in previews.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support light and dark appearance, interface scaling and keyboard navigation. Maintain legible secondary text and visible focus, selected and unread states. Leave room for long translations, addresses and subjects.
