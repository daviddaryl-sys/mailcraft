# Invite the first five MailCraft testers

Goal: five voluntary ten-minute Mac trials, including at least one Intel and one Apple Silicon Mac. These are targets, not results. Start with people you already know who enjoy trying early software. Send the invitation yourself; no automated outreach is configured.

## Personal invitation

Hi! I'm maintaining an open-source Mac mail app called MailCraft, building on the original project with account-setup and calendar/contact sync improvements. Would you try it for ten minutes and tell me where you get stuck? It is free, with downloads for Intel and Apple Silicon Macs. It is early alpha and not Apple-notarized, so macOS will show a warning. You can explore invented sample mail without connecting your real mailbox.

Downloads and the short trial guide: https://github.com/daviddaryl-sys/mailcraft/blob/main/TESTING.md

I'd especially like to know whether it opens, what feels confusing, and what would make you use it again. No pressure to connect an account or use it as your main mail app.

## Where and how to share

Start with a few personal invitations. Then share in a relevant Mac or open-source community only where its self-promotion rules allow it. Disclose that you maintain this edition. Avoid promises of complete Outlook compatibility, production readiness or universal provider support.

Ask one simple question after someone volunteers: “What was the first thing that stopped you?” Thank them, fix the most common blocker, and invite them to retry once that fix is available.

Use the GitHub tester issues to count completed trials and record install/launch blockers. GitHub download counts alone do not establish successful installations or active users. Do not ask people to post private account details.
